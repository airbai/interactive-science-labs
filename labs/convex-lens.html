06DEBRIEF

Three Images, Three Uses

u > 2f
Camera
Inverted · Reduced · Real
f < v < 2f. When the object is far away, the image forms between one and two focal lengths.
f < u < 2f
Projector
Inverted · Enlarged · Real
v > 2f. That is why the slide goes in upside down: it appears upright on the screen.
u < f
Magnifier
Upright · Enlarged · Virtual
A screen cannot catch it; look back through the lens from the other side. Image and object are on the same side.
Rule of Thumb
F splits real from virtual
2F splits big from small
Closer object, farther image
Bigger Image
u = 2f gives an image the same size as the object, which can be used to find the focal length: f = u ÷ 2.
