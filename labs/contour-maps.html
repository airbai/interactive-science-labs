07DEBRIEF

Contour Lines: Four Takeaways to Keep

01 · Principle
Same line, same height
Lines close up
The line where the water meets the slope, projected onto a plane, is a contour line
02 · Features
Bulge low: ridge
bulge high: valley
A closed loop high in the middle is a peak; between two peaks is a saddle; overlapping lines mean a cliff
03 · Slope
Sparse gentle, dense steep
profile shows relief
Cliff relative height: (n−1)d ≤ H < (n+1)d
04 · Uses
Rivers run in valleys
dams go at valley exits
Ridges are watersheds; hike gentle slopes; climb cliffs
