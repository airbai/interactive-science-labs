05EXCHANGE

Oxygenated and Deoxygenated blood

WHAT CHANGES · Where blood changes color
Oxygenated blood: rich in oxygen, bright red
Deoxygenated blood: low in oxygen, dark red (shown blue here)
Through the lungs capillaries: deoxygenated → Oxygenated blood
Through tissue capillaries: oxygenated → Deoxygenated blood
Gas exchange relies on diffusion: high concentration → low concentration
SORT · What blood flows here? Tap to switch
Oxygenated vs. deoxygenated depends on oxygen content, not on the vessel type: the pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood and the pulmonary vein carries oxygenated blood.
